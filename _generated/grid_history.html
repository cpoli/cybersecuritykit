Cryptography

cybersecuritykit.classical

Substitution and transposition ciphers, frequency analysis, Kasiski and the index of coincidence, rotor machines, and Shannon’s perfect secrecy and unicity distance.

Classical ciphers and their cryptanalysis: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.symmetric

Feistel networks and toy DES and AES, differential and linear cryptanalysis, modes of operation, padding oracles, MACs and authenticated encryption.

Block ciphers, modes and their attacks: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.pqc

Shor’s and Grover’s threat models, learning with errors, toy Kyber-style encryption, McEliece, and Lamport and Merkle hash-based signatures.

Post-quantum cryptography: breakthroughs

Protocols and identity

cybersecuritykit.protocols

Needham-Schroeder and Lowe’s attack, the Dolev-Yao attacker, Kerberos, BAN logic, and toy TLS handshakes with downgrade and replay attacks.

Security protocols: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.auth

Password entropy and guessing, salted and slow hashing from crypt to Argon2, rainbow tables, HOTP and TOTP, and challenge-response.

Authentication: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.access

Lampson’s access matrix, Bell-LaPadula and Biba, Clark-Wilson, the HRU safety problem, role-based access control, and capability systems.

Access control: breakthroughs

Systems

cybersecuritykit.software

Stack smashing and return-oriented programming on a toy VM, canaries, W^X and ASLR, fuzzing, symbolic execution, and injection attacks.

Software security: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.sidechannel

Kocher’s timing attack, simple and differential power analysis on synthetic traces, cache timing, fault attacks, and constant-time countermeasures.

Side channels: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.malware

Cohen’s undecidability result, SI and SIR worm models fitted to Morris, Code Red and Slammer, scanning strategies, botnets, and patching and quarantine.

Malware as epidemics: breakthroughs

Networks

cybersecuritykit.network

Packet filters and rule conflicts, simulated port scans, SYN floods and SYN cookies, ARP and DNS spoofing, and traceback, all on synthetic traffic.

Network security: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.detection

Denning’s IDS model, Aho-Corasick signature matching, anomaly scores, the base-rate fallacy, ROC curves, and alert correlation on synthetic logs.

Intrusion detection: breakthroughs

Data and decisions

cybersecuritykit.privacy

Re-identification by linkage, k-anonymity, l-diversity and t-closeness, randomized response, differential privacy and its composition, and membership inference.

Data privacy: breakthroughs
cybersecuritykit.risk

Attack trees and graphs, CVSS scoring, annualized loss expectancy, the Gordon-Loeb investment model, and Stackelberg security games.

Risk and economics: breakthroughs
